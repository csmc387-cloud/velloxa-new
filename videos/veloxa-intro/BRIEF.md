## Intent

A 15-second Apple Keynote-grade introduction video for Veloxa. Features high-fidelity 3D models of both a MacBook Pro and an iPhone Pro, demonstrating the live Veloxa website interface, fluid Lottie-style vector animations, interactive metrics, and an opalescent color scheme blending electric lime (#BAFF7A) and light cyan (#00FFCC) with space-silver and obsidian black (#08080a), styled with crisp white typography.

## Staging & Visual Architecture

- **Scene 1 (0.0s – 3.2s) — The Brand Ignition & Hook**: Prismatic awakening in obsidian space, radiant energy rings in lime and light cyan, geometric Lottie badge animating, kinetic headline "MEET VELOXA: WE BUILD. YOU GROW."
- **Scene 2 (3.2s – 7.5s) — 3D MacBook Pro Showcase**: Sleek Apple-esque MacBook Pro in metallic space-silver rotating in 3D perspective with realistic screen bezel and specular gloss. Displays the live Veloxa web hero, animated gradient mesh, and live counters (100% ROI Impact, 3X Traffic).
- **Scene 3 (7.5s – 11.5s) — Seamless Dual-Device Shift & 3D iPhone Pro Reveal**: MacBook tilts into background as an iPhone Pro in titanium silver glides forward in 3D space. Highlights mobile responsiveness, 24/7 automated AI intake form, and live system speed.
- **Scene 4 (11.5s – 15.0s) — Convergence & Final Sting**: Dual devices align in keynote harmony, dissolving into a brilliant lime-cyan light beam that resolves into the glowing Veloxa monogram emblem, "veloxa.io", and "SCALE YOUR VISION".
