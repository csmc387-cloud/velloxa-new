## Intent

A 15-second, Apple-keynote-style cinematic vertical brand film for "Velloxa," a digital agency. Uses the uploaded codebase as the single source of truth for all colors, typography, logos, and copy. 

Studio-lit product environment: crisp white key light, glowing rim light in exact neon lime (#BAFF7A) and electric light cyan (#00FFCC) accents, soft lens bloom, shallow depth of field, slow push-ins, and dark reflective studio floor.

## Staging & Narrative Arc (15s @ 9:16 Vertical)

- **Scene 1 (0.0s – 2.8s) — Particle Assembly**: Wordmark and official monogram emblem assembling from radiant light particles in the dark void.
- **Scene 2 (2.6s – 5.8s) — Web Engineering (3D MacBook)**: Studio-lit 3D aluminum unibody laptop rotating slowly on reflective dark ground, screen glowing with abstract code/UI in accent color (#00FFCC & #BAFF7A).
- **Scene 3 (5.6s – 8.8s) — AI Integration (3D iPhone)**: Match-cut to 3D smartphone in titanium silhouette rotating smoothly, screen pulsing with animated neural-network nodes.
- **Scene 4 (8.6s – 11.6s) — Passion Marketing & 4 Core Pillars**: Bright light-trail wipe into megaphone & growth-graph motif, branching into four glowing connected nodes: Audit → Strategy → Build → Scale.
- **Scene 5 (11.4s – 15.0s) — Climax & Brand Close**: Full wordmark centered with official vector emblem, bold tagline "WE BUILD. YOU GROW.", and stat overlay ("2X–3X Traffic Growth"), dissolving into a clean cinematic fade.
